Our Purpose & Calling

“To protect, enhance, and secure permanent community ownership of Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall Commons — ensuring these 256 acres of ancient sandstone, tranquil woodland, and rare wildlife remain free, open, and cherished for generations to come.”

The Friends of Tunbridge Wells & Rusthall Commons (Registered Charity No. 1013975) is the independent community guardian and civic champion for our historic green spaces.

🛡️

1. Secure Community Ownership

Spearheading the historic community bid to purchase the Commons freehold out of private commercial hands and vest them in permanent community trust.

🌿

2. Conserve Habitats & Wildlife

Funding ecological surveys, wetland restoration at Fir Tree Pond, veteran tree stewardship, and hands-on volunteer conservation working parties.

🥾

3. Welcome & Inspire Visitors

Acting as the digital visitor centre — publishing free illustrated trail maps, accessibility guides, family nature sheets, and guided educational walks.

Understanding Our Structure

Two Bodies, One Shared Landscape: Who Does What?

Visitors and residents often ask about the relationship between our two dedicated local organisations. We work hand-in-hand to look after the Commons:

Statutory Management Body

The Commons Conservators

Established by Act of Parliament (1890)
  • Statutory responsibility for day-to-day estate management
  • Employs the full-time Commons Ranger & maintenance team
  • Upholds Commons Byelaws, public safety, and path infrastructure
  • Funded by local council tax rates and the Manor
Official website: twcommons.org →
