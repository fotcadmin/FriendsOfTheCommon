Activities, Walks & Working Parties

Events & Guided Walks

Explore the rich natural history, sandstone geology, and heritage of our commons with expert-led walks and hands-on conservation sessions. Each event clearly indicates the organising body.

Calendar of Events

Upcoming Activities on the Commons

All events and walks are free and open to everyone in the local community.

Oct 01
Guided Walk & Talk • Tunbridge Wells Common

Let’s Talk About Fungi: Walk & Talk

🗓️ Thursday 1 October • ⏰ 10:00am – 1:00pm • 📍 Meeting at Upper Pantiles Car Park

Join fungi expert Martin Allison for a walk and talk exploring the fascinating fungi of Tunbridge Wells Common, learning how to identify different species and discovering more about the vital role they play in our landscape.

Essential Details: Please wear suitable footwear for damp weather. Magnifying glass and notebook optional. Note: this is not a foraging event; no fungi will be collected or eaten.

Oct 03
Volunteer Conservation • Rusthall Common

Volunteer Working Party: Rusthall Common

🗓️ Saturday 3 October • ⏰ 10:00am – 12:00pm • 📍 Happy Valley Entrance (St Paul’s Church)

Join Friends of the Commons for a morning of litter picking and conservation work on Rusthall Common, followed by tea, coffee, and refreshments at the Lodge by St Paul’s Church.

Essential Details: Everyone welcome, including families. Sturdy gardening gloves and outdoor footwear recommended. Tools and guidance provided.

Oct 18
Geology & Heritage Tour • TW Common

Ancient Sandstone & Geotrail Discovery Tour

🗓️ Sunday 18 October • ⏰ 2:00pm – 4:00pm • 📍 Wellington Rocks Guide Post

Discover 135 million years of Ardingly sandstone history. Learn how prehistoric ice, wind, and water sculpted Wellington Rocks and how rare lichens and bee colonies thrive on these ancient crags.

Essential Details: Gentle walking terrain with optional rock scrambles. Illustrated geology field guides provided free of charge.
Book Free Tour →
Nov 07
Pond Habitat & Ecology • Rusthall Common

Marlpit Ponds & Wetland Habitat Morning

🗓️ Saturday 7 November • ⏰ 10:00am – 12:30pm • 📍 Marlpit Ponds Dipping Platform

A hands-on conservation and survey morning focusing on the historic Marlpit Ponds. Learn about great crested newts, aquatic dragonfly larvae, and help clear encroaching reeds to preserve open water.

Essential Details: Wellies or waterproof boots required. Pond-dipping trays and identification charts provided for all participants.

Nov 21
Bird Survey & Ornithology • Higher Common

Winter Bird Walk & Dawn Census

🗓️ Saturday 21 November • ⏰ 8:00am – 10:30am • 📍 Mount Edgcumbe Steps

Join our volunteer ornithologists to record winter migrants across the heather glades and mature beech woodlands. Spot redwings, fieldfares, siskins, and resident woodpeckers.

Essential Details: Bring binoculars if available (spares provided). Warm layers and flask recommended. Suitable for beginner and experienced birders.

Woodland Stewardship • TW & Rusthall

Veteran Tree Survey & Winter Care Session

🗓️ Saturday 12 December • ⏰ 9:30am – 12:30pm • 📍 Queen Victoria Grove

Learn how we survey and protect our 200+ year old veteran oaks, sweet chestnuts, and beeches planted after the Great Storm. Includes deadwood habitat mulching and sapling protection.

Friends of the Commons Tunbridge Wells & Rusthall

The Friends of Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall Commons is an independent registered charity (No. 1013975) dedicated to fundraising, community ownership, and protecting 256 acres of historic common land.

Working in active partnership with The Commons Conservators, the statutory management body under the 1890 Act.
