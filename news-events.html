Dispatches from the Field

Spring Wildlife Walk: Survey Highlights & Observations

By Michael Blencowe • Naturalist & Walk Leader

A fantastic turnout of local champions joined us across the Higher Common and Happy Valley. We were delighted to observe healthy colonies of native woodland pollinators taking advantage of the early warmth.

Over the course of two hours, walk participants spotted green woodpeckers drumming in the veteran sweet chestnuts, brimstone butterflies navigating along woodland clearings, and emerging wood anemones carpeting undisturbed pockets of ancient soil.

The ongoing winter work by volunteer working parties to selectively clear choking bracken and rhododendron has noticeably improved light levels on the ground, directly encouraging the natural regeneration of native heather and bilberry.
