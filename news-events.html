From the Field

Wildlife Walk: Highlights & Species Survey

By Michael Blencowe • Guided Nature Walk Leader

A fantastic crowd of local residents joined us for our spring wildlife walk across the Higher Common and Happy Valley. We were delighted to observe healthy colonies of native woodland pollinators taking advantage of the early warmth.

Over the course of two hours, participants spotted green woodpeckers drumming in the veteran sweet chestnuts, brimstone butterflies cruising along the woodland rides, and emerging wood anemones carpeting undisturbed pockets of ancient soil.

The ongoing work by volunteer working parties to selectively clear choking bracken and rhododendron has noticeably improved light levels on the ground, encouraging natural regeneration of native heather and bilberry.
