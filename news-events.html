Guided Walk & Talk • Tunbridge Wells Common

Let’s Talk About Fungi: Walk & Talk

Thursday 1 October • 10:00am – 1:00pm • Meeting at Upper Pantiles (Fairground) Car Park

Join fungi expert Martin Allison for a walk and talk exploring the fascinating fungi of Tunbridge Wells Common, learning how to identify different species and discovering more about the vital role they play in our landscape.

Essential Details: Please wear suitable footwear and come prepared for damp weather. A small magnifying glass and notebook are optional. Please note: this is not a foraging event and no fungi will be collected or eaten. Bring your own drink and snack. The Upper Pantiles car park is chargeable (or use the free car park on Fir Tree Road).

Volunteer Conservation • Rusthall Common

Volunteer Day: Rusthall Common

Saturday 3 October • 10:00am – 12:00pm • Meet at Happy Valley Entrance (by War Memorial / St Paul’s Church)

Join Friends of the Commons for a morning of litter picking and conservation work on Rusthall Common, followed by tea and coffee at the Lodge by St Paul’s Church.

Essential Details: Everyone is welcome, including children accompanied by a responsible adult. No need to stay for the full two hours. Please bring suitable outdoor clothing, sturdy gardening gloves, a drink and snack, and your own litter picker if you have one. We’ll provide the rest!

Friends of the Commons Tunbridge Wells & Rusthall

The Friends of Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall Commons is an independent registered charity (No. 1013975) dedicated to fundraising, community ownership, and protecting 256 acres of historic common land.

Working in active partnership with The Commons Conservators, the statutory management body under the 1890 Act.
