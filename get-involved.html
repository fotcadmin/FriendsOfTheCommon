Charitable Giving

Make a Donation with Gift Aid

One-off and regular donations fund urgent tree surgery following winter gales, pond restoration, and legal representation. If you are a UK taxpayer, Gift Aid increases the value of your donation by 25% at no extra cost to you.

Online Donation Form

How your gift directly protects the land

  • £15: Purchases heavy-duty loppers and protective gloves for volunteer work parties.
  • £50: Funds stone resurfacing materials to restore eroded woodland walking paths and steps.
  • £150: Enables specialist ecological surveys for bats, rare fungi, and native pollinators.
  • £500: Contributes directly to veteran tree management and emergency storm clearance.

Legacy Giving in Your Will

Hands-on Conservation

Join Our Volunteer Working Parties

A rewarding way to spend a morning outdoors, meet fellow nature enthusiasts, and care directly for the landscape. No prior experience is required—full guidance and tools are provided.

Habitat Conservation Working Parties

Regular Saturday morning sessions clearing invasive scrub, pulling encroaching birch, and restoring rare lowland heathland glades.

Community Litter Clearance

Monthly coordinated sweeps keeping pathways, pond margins, and picnic glades pristine and safe for grazing animals and wildlife.

Charity Committee & Event Roles

Help organise guided nature walks, write newsletter articles, assist with social media, or contribute to charity governance.

Friends of the Commons

The Friends of Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall Commons is a registered charity dedicated to protecting 256 acres of common land for wildlife, heritage, and the public.

Registered Charity No. 1013975 • In active partnership with the Tunbridge Wells Commons Conservators under the 1890 Act.
