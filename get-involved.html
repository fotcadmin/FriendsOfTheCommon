Annual Membership

Join the Friends of the Commons

Subscriptions provide the stable, reliable income we need to fund ongoing conservation projects, habitat restoration, and trail improvements year after year.

What membership gives you:

  • ✅ Welcome Pack: Free printed trail maps of Tunbridge Wells & Rusthall Commons.
  • ✅ Regular Newsletters: In-depth updates and seasonal reports from the Ranger & Natural History specialist.
  • ✅ Priority Booking & Discounts: Early access to popular social events, bat nights, and guided nature walks.
  • ✅ Direct Impact: Funds essential tools, tree safety works, and pond clearance.

Hands-on Conservation

Join our volunteer working parties

A fantastic way to stay active outdoors, meet fellow nature-lovers, and help protect the commons. No previous experience needed — tools, gloves, training, and friendly supervision are provided!

🌲 Practical Habitat Conservation

Clearing invasive rhododendron and birch scrub to restore endangered lowland heathland habitat.

🧹 Monthly Litter Picks

Keeping clearings, glades, ponds, and children’s play areas safe and clean.

🤝 Committee & Event Roles

Help organise community walks, write newsletter pieces, or assist with charity governance.
