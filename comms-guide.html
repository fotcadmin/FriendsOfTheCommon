Website Content Management Guide

Prepared specifically for the Head of Comms. This guide explains how the modernized site is structured, how to perform routine updates in minutes, and key governance best practices.

1. The 6-Pillar Information Architecture

The previous website suffered from 20 scattered, overlapping pages (e.g. separate /join-us, /sign-up, /support-us, /donate, and /volunteer). We have unified the site into 6 purposeful sections:

  • 🌲 1. Home: The shop window — campaign alert, headline stats, core pillars, public endorsements, and event teasers.
  • 📢 2. Campaign & Sale: The community bid, media coverage (BBC/Guardian), legal key facts, sale FAQs, and financial pledge form.
  • 🗺️ 3. Explore & Maps: Digital Visitor Centre, parking directory & council disclaimer, interactive Leaflet trail map with category filters, downloadable maps, and wildlife guides.
  • 🤝 4. Get Involved: Direct Debit membership (GoCardless), one-off donations (+25% Gift Aid calculator), and volunteer sign-ups.
  • 📅 5. News & Events: Upcoming guided walks, volunteer dates, ranger updates, and photo gallery.
  • 🏛️ 6. About & Contact: 1987 storm history, Conservators partnership, downloadable PDFs (Constitution, Safeguarding), and contact form.

2. Routine Day-to-Day Comms Workflows

A Adding a Nature Walk or Event

All events live on the News & Events page in pre-formatted responsive cards with calendar date badges.

Key fields needed: Event Title, Month & Day, Category (e.g. Nature Walk, Fungi Foray, Bat Evening), Short Description, and whether booking is required.

B Updating Volunteer Working Party Dates

Volunteer dates are prominently featured on both the Home and Get Involved pages.

Pro-Tip: Always mention that tools, gloves, and friendly supervision are provided to reassure first-time volunteers!

C Uploading New Documents or Committee Minutes

Official governance documents (Constitution, Safeguarding Policy, Accounts) are located in the assets/docs/ directory and linked from the About & Contact page and site footer.

3. Charity Commission Compliance: Trustee Best Practice

Under the UK Charities Act 2011 (Section 185), because you are a trustee and Alistair is your spouse (a legally defined "connected person"):

  1. Declaration of Interest: At any committee meeting discussing website rebuild proposals, quotes, or fees, declare your relationship and step out of the room.
  2. Minuted Decision: The remaining independent trustees must minute that hiring Alistair at a discounted charity rate represents best value for the charity.
  3. Written Agreement: A simple 1-page agreement specifying scope, fee, and milestones must be signed by another trustee.

Following this straightforward procedure protects your position as a trustee and guarantees 100% compliance with Charity Commission regulations.

4. Division of Responsibilities

Head of Comms (Content)

  • Publishing walk & event notices
  • Writing Ranger reports & news stories
  • Sharing updates to social media / press
  • Reviewing volunteer & contact messages

Technical Webmaster (Code)

  • Hosting & domain DNS management
  • SSL certificates & security encryption
  • Payment gateway & Gift Aid compliance
  • Performance & mobile compatibility checks
