Community Campaign • Autumn 2026

Community Freehold Acquisition Bid Gains Overwhelming Support

Over 3,000 local residents and conservation groups have registered their formal backing for The Friends' bid to acquire the freehold of Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall Commons. The bid guarantees perpetual charitable protection under democratic community governance.

Ecology Survey • Late Summer 2026

Fungi Survey Confirms Rare Waxcap Species on Rusthall Common

A recent biological survey led by local naturalists documented significant colonies of Hygrocybe (waxcap) fungi on the undisturbed acidic turf of Rusthall Common, indicating centuries of unploughed grassland habitat of regional ecological significance.

Habitat Restoration • Summer 2026

Marlpit Pond Restoration Project Enters Next Phase

Working alongside the Commons Conservators, Friends volunteers have cleared invasive overgrowth and re-established sunlight corridors to Marlpit Pond, encouraging the return of native dragonflies, smooth newts, and aquatic flora.

Volunteer Work Parties Restore Eroded Woodland Paths

Friends work party volunteers completed essential repairs along key pedestrian routes connecting the Upper Cricket Ground to Happy Valley, improving drainage and protecting fragile tree roots from heavy footfall.

Friends of the Commons Tunbridge Wells & Rusthall

The Friends of Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall Commons is an independent registered charity (No. 1013975) dedicated to fundraising, community ownership, and protecting 256 acres of historic common land.

Working in active partnership with The Commons Conservators, the statutory management body under the 1890 Act.
