Community Support

What does your membership support?

The funds we raise have financed many projects over the years, such as major clearance, new paths, purchasing of tools and key pieces of equipment.

Some of our members join the committee, and some take an active part in practical conservation and volunteer work. From litter-picking and clearance of invasive species to pond and rock clearance, your support helps protect this landscape for everyone.

Charitable Giving

Make a Donation with Gift Aid

One-off and regular donations fund urgent storm repairs, pond restoration, and wildlife surveys. If you are a UK taxpayer, Gift Aid increases the value of your donation by 25% at no extra cost to you.

Practical Conservation & Support

Volunteering on the Commons

Practical conservation on the Commons is organised and led by The Commons Conservators, caring for the land under the 1890 Act. The Friends actively support these efforts, funding tools and equipment and encouraging community involvement.

Community volunteers caring for the landscape

Practical Conservation Mornings

From litter-picking and invasive species clearance to scrub control and pond care, volunteer mornings offer a friendly way to spend time outdoors while making a tangible difference to our commons.

Tools and guidance provided by the Commons Ranger team.
Get Involved

The Friends of Tunbridge Wells & Rusthall Commons

The Friends of Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall Commons is an independent registered charity (No. 1013975) dedicated to fundraising, community ownership, and protecting 256 acres of historic common land.

Working in active partnership with The Commons Conservators, caring for the land under the 1890 Act.
