Insects & Minibeasts

Wood Ant

Watch for busy trails along sandy footpaths and dry tree roots.

Bumblebee

Busy foraging among purple heather, clover, and blackberry blossoms.

Common Wasp

Yellow and black stripes; important garden predators and pollinators.

Garden Spider

Look for circular orb webs spun between bracken stems and gorse bushes.

Shield Bug

Shaped like a medieval knight's shield; resting on sunny hazel leaves.

Earthworm

Found under damp autumn leaves and moist soil along wooded trails.

Common Fly

Basking on warm sandstone rocks around Wellington Rocks.

Hoverfly

Looks like a wasp but harmless; hovers motionless in mid-air!

Centipede

Fast-moving hunter with dozens of legs, hiding in dark rotting bark.

Butterflies

Peacock Butterfly

Large velvety red wings with dazzling purple-blue eye-spots to startle predators.

Red Admiral

Velvety black wings with bright orange-red bands and white wingtip spots.

Small Tortoiseshell

Orange and black pattern with a lovely row of blue crescent spots on wing edges.

Small Copper

Bright fiery copper-orange wings with dark spots; loves sunny heathland paths.

Brimstone

Bright butter-yellow wings shaped just like leaves when closed.

Comma

Ragged wing edges and a tiny white comma mark on the brown underside.

Holly Blue

Bright silvery-blue wings fluttering high around holly and ivy bushes in spring.

Meadow Brown

Warm brown wings with an orange patch and black eye-spot; dances over summer grass.
