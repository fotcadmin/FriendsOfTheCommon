The Facts About The Sale

Why are the Commons on the market?
The current commercial owner (Targetfollow) placed the freehold of both Commons up for disposal. The Friends have stepped forward with a credible £1m+ community purchase proposal to secure permanent local guardianship.
Can a private purchaser build houses or fence it off?
No. The 256 acres are protected under the Commons Act 2006 and County of Kent Act 1981. Byelaws and day-to-day management are governed by the statutory Commons Conservators (est. 1890). However, private ownership brings ongoing risk of neglect, commercial disputes, and lack of conservation investment.
Why is community ownership the right answer?
Placing the freehold in charitable community stewardship guarantees that 100% of future resources focus solely on wildlife, sandstone preservation, volunteer working parties, and public enjoyment for the next five centuries.
