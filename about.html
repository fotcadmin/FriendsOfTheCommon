About The Friends, Governance & Contact

Founded following the Great Storm of 1987, The Friends work in close partnership with the statutory Commons Conservators to protect 256 acres of historic landscape.

Our Founding Purpose

“To protect, enhance, and secure permanent community ownership of Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall Commons — ensuring these 256 acres remain free, open, and cherished for generations to come.”

The Friends of Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall Commons is the independent registered charity (No. 1013975), membership body, and fundraising arm for our shared commons.

Pillar 1

Secure Community Ownership

Leading the community bid to buy the Commons freehold out of commercial hands and vest it into permanent charitable trust.

Pillar 2

Conserve Habitats & Wildlife

Funding ecological surveys, wetland restoration at Fir Tree Pond, veteran tree stewardship, and volunteer working parties.

Pillar 3

Welcome & Inspire Visitors

Publishing free illustrated trail maps, accessibility guides, family nature spotter sheets, and expert guided nature walks.

Charity Origins

Dedicated to the Commons Since 1991

The Friends were established in 1991 following the widespread destruction caused across southern England by the Great Storm of October 1987. Hundreds of ancient beech and oak trees across Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall Commons were flattened overnight.

Local residents came together to support clearance, tree replanting, and ensure the commons recovered. Over the past three decades, our charity has evolved into an essential community institution, raising funds for practical equipment, tree safety, and ecological conservation.

Our Partnership with the Commons Conservators

The statutory day-to-day management of the Commons lies with the Commons Conservators, constituted by Act of Parliament in 1890 comprising representatives of the Freeholder, the Town Council, and Rusthall Parish Council. The Friends fund enhancements outside their statutory budget, such as specialist surveys, trail restoration, and volunteer working parties.
