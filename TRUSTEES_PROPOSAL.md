# Modernising the Friends of the Commons Digital Presence
## A Strategic Proposal for the Board of Trustees
**The Friends of Tunbridge Wells & Rusthall Commons (Registered Charity No. 1013975)**  
*Prepared for the Trustees' Meeting — Monday Afternoon*

---

### Executive Summary
The Commons represent 256 acres of irreplaceable green space, ancient sandstone formations, and living civic heritage in the heart of Royal Tunbridge Wells and Rusthall. As the charity embarks on the historic campaign to secure freehold community ownership, our digital presence must serve as our most reliable, secure, and persuasive community asset.

At present, our website suffers from fragile administrative workflows, mobile usability friction, and structural risks that undermine public confidence and consume disproportionate volunteer time. 

This proposal outlines a focused modernisation of the charity's website for a fixed one-off investment of **£1,000**, with **£0 ongoing annual software fees**, structured around **three strategic pillars**.

---

### Pillar 1: Eliminating Administrative Friction & Empowering Volunteers
*From fragile page builders to stress-free, unbreakable publishing.*

* **The Problem:** The current site relies on a brittle WordPress & Elementor configuration. Updating a simple news article or event requires navigating complex design layers. A minor edit can accidentally misalign page layouts or break global stylesheets (as recently occurred with missing styling files that disrupted public access). This creates acute anxiety for administrative volunteers, discourages regular updates, and requires external technical intervention for basic upkeep.
* **The Modernised Solution:** We will implement an intuitive, dedicated editorial dashboard (customised for charities).
  * **Form-Based Simplicity:** Trustees and volunteers update News, Notices, and Events by simply typing into clearly labelled boxes (Title, Date, Body, Image) and clicking "Publish".
  * **Unbreakable Formatting:** Volunteers literally *cannot* break the design, font hierarchy, or mobile responsiveness. The layout is decoupled from the content.
  * **Zero Maintenance Overhead:** Eliminates fragile plugins, security update vulnerabilities, and costly monthly maintenance retainers.

---

### Pillar 2: Driving Community Traffic, Discoverability & Local Economy Integration
*Transforming a static noticeboard into the go-to pocket guide for the Commons.*

* **The Problem:** The existing website functions primarily as a static brochure. Mobile visitors—who make up over 70% of web traffic—struggle to find practical visitor information (accessible walking trails, parking near Wellington Rocks or Toad Rock, dog-walking guidelines, and family-friendly routes).
* **The Modernised Solution:**
  * **Interactive Mobile-First Commons Guide:** Clear, fast-loading trail maps, heritage spotters' guides, and parking directions optimised for walkers using smartphones on the Common.
  * **Civic & Tourism Integration:** Dedicated trails connecting the Commons with the Spa Valley Railway, High Rocks, The Pantiles, and local heritage pubs, encouraging local business partnerships and town-wide pride.
  * **Search Engine Dominance (SEO):** Clean, fast code ensures that anyone searching for *"things to do in Tunbridge Wells"*, *"Tunbridge Wells circular walks"*, or *"Toad Rock parking"* lands directly on the Friends' website, introducing hundreds of new residents to the charity each month.

---

### Pillar 3: Growing Memberships, Donations & Strategic Community Pledges
*Frictionless revenue generation with strictly safeguarded commercial leverage.*

* **The Problem:** The current donation and membership sign-up journey is clunky on mobile devices, leading to drop-offs before completion. Furthermore, public campaign fundraising must balance community mobilisation with commercial discretion.
* **The Modernised Solution:**
  * **One-Touch Mobile Sign-ups:** Modern, rapid checkout for annual £15 memberships and one-off donations with integrated Gift Aid declaration—allowing supporters to join in under 30 seconds using Apple Pay, Google Pay, or debit card.
  * **Frictionless Pledge Collection:** An accessible, reassuring pledge flow starting at £20 that registers resident commitment without taking funds upfront.
  * **Strategic Commercial Confidentiality Safeguard:** Crucially, the public-facing campaign will **not** expose running pledge sums, total targets, or average pledge values. This directly protects the Trustees' negotiating position with Targetfollow and commercial vendors, preventing counterparties from estimating the charity’s acquisition budget or maximum financial capacity, while still building an overwhelming mandate of town-wide solidarity.
